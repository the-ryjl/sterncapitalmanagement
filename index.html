How we invest

See the book →

Fundamental long/short equity. Three kinds of ideas make it into the book.

Long

Quality compounders

Strong businesses with pricing power and high returns on capital. We buy when the price doesn't reflect how long they can keep growing.

Short

Overpriced stories

Stocks priced for growth or margins the company can't deliver. We short when the gap is big and something is likely to close it.

Long

Beaten-up names with a catalyst

Good companies that sold off more than the fundamentals justify. We buy when an event in the next few quarters, like earnings or a cost cut, can turn the stock around.

How the pods work

Technology: depth before breadth

Each analyst owns one subsector with no overlap: software, fintech & payments, internet, semiconductors, media, digital infrastructure. As the pod grows, big subsectors become teams without changing the structure.

Fixed Income & Credit: beat the AGG

Every rates and credit call, from investment grade through distressed, will be measured against the Bloomberg U.S. Aggregate, tracked daily with AGG.
