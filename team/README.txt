Team photos go in this folder.

Name each file after the person, lowercase, words joined by dashes, .jpg:
  lily-zawadskas.jpg   nikita-makaed.jpg   matthew-richardson.jpg

Square-ish headshots work best (at least 300 x 300 px). Faces are framed
from slightly above centre. Anyone without a photo shows their initials.
To add or rename people, edit EXEC, PMS or ADVISORS near the top of the
script in index.html.
